704|How do I add someone on WIPP?
705|Three ways: scan their WIPP QR code, search for their @username, or use WIPP Touch by bringing your two phones together. Your phone number is never shared.
706|I’m not getting the SMS code
707|Check the country (dialling code) and your number, wait 45 seconds, then tap “Resend the code”. If nothing arrives, write to us from the Support tab with your country and your carrier.
708|I forgot my lock code
709|Go to Me › Settings › Privacy › Lock code, then tap “Forgot the code?”. WIPP asks for Face ID or your iPhone passcode to confirm that it is you, then you create a new code. Same for WIPP Privé.
710|How do I lock a conversation?
711|First create your code in Me › Settings › Privacy › Lock code. Then press and hold the conversation in Chats (or ⋯ in the conversation) and choose Lock. Its content and notifications are hidden.
712|I changed phones: where are my old messages?
713|Your messages are end-to-end encrypted with a key that stays on your phone. On a new phone, your contacts, groups and profile are there, but old messages show as “encrypted for another device”. New messages read normally.
714|How do I change my name or my @username?
715|Me › Edit my profile. Your @username can change if it is available; the old one is then released and your old profile links or QR code stop working.
716|How do I create my business page?
717|Me › My business card. Add the name, category, city, a cover, a logo, your photos, opening hours, services and social networks. Your page gets its own QR code, different from your personal QR, and customers write to you in a separate “Business” conversation.
718|How do I report or block someone?
719|In a conversation, a story, a profile, a group, a listing or a business page, tap ⋯ then “Report” or “Block”. Reported content disappears for you right away, and the WIPP team reviews every report.
